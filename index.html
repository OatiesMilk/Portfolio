About Me

I'm a full-stack web developer with a passion for building web applications that are simple to use and easy to maintain. I studied Information Technology and have spent the last few years working on projects that range from small business websites to internal tools that automate repetitive work.

My goal is to keep growing as a developer by working on projects that solve real problems, while writing code that the next person (often future me) can understand at a glance. I care about clean UI, accessible markup, and code that doesn't need a manual to modify.

Outside of coding, I enjoy exploring UI/UX design trends, contributing to small open-source tools, and continuously learning new parts of the web platform.
